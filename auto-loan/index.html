Understanding Auto Loan Costs by State

The true cost of buying a car varies significantly by state. Beyond the sticker price, you need to account for:

  • Sales tax: Ranges from 0% (Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire, Oregon) to 8.25% (Nevada). This alone can add thousands to your total cost.
  • Registration fees: Range from $14 (Mississippi) to $225 (Florida). Some states base fees on vehicle weight or value.
  • Interest rates: Average rates vary by state due to local market conditions, credit union availability, and dealer competition.
  • Insurance costs: Mandatory in all states (except NH), and premiums vary widely by location.
