Tips to Get the Best Auto Loan Rate in Louisiana

  1. Check your credit score first. Scores above 720 qualify for the best rates (often 3-5% APR).
  2. Get pre-approved. Apply at your bank, credit union, or online lender before visiting the dealership.
  3. Compare at least 3 lenders. Louisiana credit unions often offer rates 1-2% lower than big banks.
  4. Keep the loan term short. 48 or 60 months saves thousands vs. 72 or 84 months.
  5. Put 20% down. Avoids being "upside down" on the loan and reduces total interest.
  6. Watch for dealer markups. Dealers can add 1-3% to your rate. Always compare with pre-approval.
  7. Budget for 4.45% sales tax. On a $30,800 car, that's $1,371 extra.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average auto loan rate in Louisiana?

The average auto loan rate in Louisiana for a 60-month new car loan is approximately 7.2% APR for borrowers with good credit (700+). Rates vary by credit score — excellent credit may qualify for 4-5%, while subprime borrowers may see 10-15%+.

How much is sales tax on a car in Louisiana?

Louisiana charges 4.45% sales tax on vehicle purchases. On a $30,800 vehicle, the sales tax would be $1,371. Some local jurisdictions may add additional tax.

How much should I put down on a car in Louisiana?

Financial experts recommend putting 20% down on a new car and 10% on a used car. On the average Louisiana vehicle price of $30,800, that's $6,160 down. This helps you avoid negative equity and reduces your monthly payment significantly.

Should I finance through a dealer or bank in Louisiana?

Compare both options. Louisiana credit unions often offer the lowest rates (sometimes 1-2% below dealer financing). Get pre-approved from your bank or credit union first, then see if the dealer can beat it. Some dealers offer 0% APR promotional rates on select models.

Is it better to buy new or used in Louisiana?

Used cars typically offer better value. A 2-3 year old car saves 30-40% off MSRP while retaining modern features. However, new cars in Louisiana may qualify for manufacturer incentives, lower interest rates, and full warranty coverage. Consider total cost of ownership including 4.45% sales tax and insurance.
