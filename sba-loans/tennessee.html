💡 Tips for Getting an SBA Loan in Tennessee

  • Credit Score: Aim for 680+ for best rates. Some programs accept 620+.
  • Business Plan: Required for all SBA loans. Include financial projections, market analysis.
  • Collateral: SBA 7(a) loans over $350K require collateral. Real estate is preferred.
  • Time in Business: 2+ years preferred. Startups may qualify for Microloans.
  • Revenue: Show consistent revenue growth. Lenders want 1.25x debt service coverage ratio.
  • Local Resources: Visit your Tennessee SBDC (Small Business Development Center) for free counseling.

❓ Tennessee SBA Loan FAQ

What is the current SBA loan rate in Tennessee?

SBA 7(a) variable rates are typically Prime + 2.25% to 2.75%. As of 2026, this ranges from 10.00% to 11.00%. SBA 504 loans offer fixed rates, currently around 5.5% to 7%.

How long does it take to get an SBA loan in Tennessee?

SBA 7(a): 30-90 days. SBA Express: 36 hours for approval (funding in 2-4 weeks). SBA 504: 60-120 days. Microloans: 2-4 weeks through local intermediaries.

What can I use an SBA loan for in Tennessee?

Working capital, equipment, real estate, inventory, debt refinancing, business acquisition, franchise fees, and leasehold improvements. The Healthcare industry is Tennessee's strongest sector for SBA lending.

Do I need a down payment for an SBA loan?

SBA 7(a): Typically 10-20% down. SBA 504: 10% down (borrower) + 40% CDC + 50% bank. Microloans: Often no down payment required.

What are the SBA guaranty fees?

Loans ≤$150K: 2%. Loans $150K-$700K: 3%. Loans $700K-$1M: 3.5%. Loans >$1M: 3.75%. These fees are typically rolled into the loan.
