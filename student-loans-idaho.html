🏛️ Idaho Student Loan Forgiveness Programs

Idaho Rural Physician Incentive Program.

  • Federal PSLF: Work for a qualifying Idaho government or nonprofit employer for 10 years → remaining balance forgiven (tax-free)
  • Income-Driven Forgiveness: After 20-25 years of IDR payments, remaining balance forgiven
  • Teacher Loan Forgiveness: Up to $17,500 forgiven after 5 years teaching in low-income Idaho schools
  • State Programs: Idaho Rural Physician Incentive Program

📋 Student Loans in Idaho: What You Need to Know

How much student debt does the average Idaho graduate have?

The average student loan debt for Idaho graduates is approximately $28,040, which is near the national average of ~$29,400. With an average starting salary of $44,910 and a cost of living index of 93%, Idaho graduates benefit from lower living costs that can help accelerate repayment.

What repayment plan is best for Idaho graduates?

Given lower average salaries in Idaho, income-driven plans (IBR, PAYE, SAVE) may offer significant payment relief. Use the calculator above to compare all options with your specific numbers.

Can I get my student loans forgiven in Idaho?

Yes! Besides federal programs (PSLF, Teacher Forgiveness), Idaho offers state-specific programs: Idaho Rural Physician Incentive Program. Check eligibility requirements as they vary by profession and location within the state.

How does Idaho's cost of living affect repayment?

Idaho's cost of living index is 93% of the national average. This moderate cost of living means standard repayment is usually manageable for most graduates.
