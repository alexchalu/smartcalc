About Hawaii Paycheck Taxes

Hawaii has a graduated state income tax with rates of 1.4-11%. The top marginal rate is 11%. Your paycheck will be reduced by federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security (6.2%), and Medicare (1.45%).

Deductions Applied

  • Federal Income Tax: Based on 2026 brackets with standard deduction ($15,000 single, $30,000 married, $22,500 head of household)
  • Hawaii State Tax: 1.4-11%
  • Social Security (FICA): 6.2% on income up to $176,100
  • Medicare: 1.45% on all income (+ 0.9% additional Medicare tax on income over $200K single / $250K married)
  • 401(k) contributions reduce your taxable income for federal and state taxes

Frequently Asked Questions

How is take-home pay calculated in Hawaii?

Take-home pay is your gross salary minus all deductions: federal income tax, Hawaii state income tax, Social Security (6.2%), Medicare (1.45%), and any pre-tax deductions like 401(k) contributions and health insurance premiums.

What is the Hawaii state income tax rate?

Hawaii has a graduated income tax structure with rates ranging from 1.4-11%. Higher income is taxed at progressively higher rates.

How much does Social Security take from my paycheck?

Social Security tax is 6.2% of your gross pay, up to the wage base limit of $176,100 in 2026. Your employer also pays a matching 6.2%. Income above the limit is not subject to Social Security tax.

Does a 401(k) contribution reduce my taxes?

Yes! Traditional 401(k) contributions are pre-tax, meaning they reduce your taxable income for both federal and state income tax purposes. This can significantly lower your tax burden while building retirement savings.

How accurate is this paycheck calculator?

This calculator provides a close estimate based on 2026 federal and Hawaii state tax brackets. Actual results may vary based on local taxes, additional deductions, tax credits, and your specific situation. Consult a tax professional for precise figures.
