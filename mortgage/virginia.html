Buying a Home in Virginia — What You Need to Know

Virginia's average home price is approximately $400,000, with a property tax rate of 0.82% (effective rate). That translates to roughly $273/month in property taxes on the average home.

Homeowner's insurance in Virginia averages about $1,600/year ($133/month). This is close to the national average for homeowner's insurance costs.

Key Virginia Mortgage Facts

  • Property Tax Rate: 0.82% effective rate
  • Average Home Price: $400,000
  • Average Insurance: $1,600/year
  • 20% Down Payment: $80,000
  • PMI: Required if down payment is less than 20%

Tip: If you put less than 20% down on a conventional loan, you'll pay Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI), typically 0.5-1% of the loan amount annually. FHA loans require mortgage insurance for the life of the loan. VA loans have no PMI requirement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good mortgage rate in Virginia in 2026?

Mortgage rates vary by lender, credit score, and loan type. As of 2026, average 30-year fixed rates hover around 6.5-7%. Shopping multiple lenders can save you thousands over the life of your loan.

How much house can I afford in Virginia?

A common guideline is the 28/36 rule: your mortgage payment shouldn't exceed 28% of gross monthly income, and total debt payments shouldn't exceed 36%. With Virginia's average home price of $400,000, you'd typically need a household income of $500,000+ per year.

Should I get a 15-year or 30-year mortgage in Virginia?

A 15-year mortgage has higher monthly payments but saves significantly on interest. For a $400,000 home with 20% down, you could save over $100,000 in interest with a 15-year vs 30-year loan.

What are closing costs in Virginia?

Closing costs in Virginia typically range from 2-5% of the home price. On a $400,000 home, expect to pay approximately $12,000 in closing costs.
